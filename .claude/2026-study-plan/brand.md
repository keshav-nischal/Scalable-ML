# Reputation & LinkedIn Playbook

**Thesis:** content-led presence self-selects qualified inbound and converts **4–6× better than cold outreach.** You don't need to go viral — you need ~50 consistent, credible posts over 6 months that make the *right* people think *"this person ships reliable LLM systems."* Consistency for years beats intensity for months.

**Your two unfair differentiators — lead with both, everywhere:**
1. **GSoC 2024 contributor** — public merged PRs = instant trust. Label it *"Google Summer of Code 2024 Contributor, [Org]"* (org as affiliation, never "Google" as employer).
2. **ex-SDET** — reframed as *"the ML engineer who tests, evaluates, and hardens models for production."* Reliability/eval is exactly what LLM buyers fear getting wrong, and almost no competitor can claim it.

---

## Profile = landing page, not résumé

- **Headline:** `Building reliable LLM/ML systems | RAG · LLM Evaluation · MLOps | ex-SDET · GSoC 2024 Contributor | Open to freelance`
- **Featured (3 pinned proofs):** best repo (P3 RAG API), a reproduction writeup (nanoGPT), a quantified project (P4 eval pipeline). Update as you ship.
- **About:** open with a *client pain* ("Most LLM demos work in the notebook and break in production…"), state what you do, end with a **discovery-call CTA**.
- **Experience:** SDET framed as ML-adjacent (test automation, quality systems, reliability). GSoC with the org + a link to merged PRs.

## Content engine (never run out of ideas)

**3 pillars × 10 angles = 30 seeds.** Jot one seed per study session; you'll always have a queue.

**Pillars:** (1) Technical depth (what you learned) · (2) Learning-in-public (what you built/broke) · (3) Freelance & industry takes (what it means for teams).

**Angles:** Tip · Stat · Step-by-step · Lesson · Benefit · Reason · Mistake · Example · Question · Personal story.

> Example seeds: *Mistake* × depth → "The RAG bug that silently tanked my faithfulness score." *Step* × learning → "How I reproduced GPT-2 in a weekend." *Stat* × industry → "85% of ML projects never reach production. Here's the gap I'm training for."

---

## Formats & algorithm (2026)

- **PDF carousels / document posts** = highest engagement (drive saves + reposts, the strongest reach signals). Make these your milestone format.
- **First-person "How I…" case studies with quantified outcomes** beat generic AI explainers and attract qualified inbound.
- Strong **first-150-char hook**; put external **links in the first comment** (not the post body).
- **Avoid** polls and "Agree? comment 👇" bait (spam-classified). Treat video as secondary; carousels primary.
- **Comments count as micro-posts** — LinkedIn surfaces comment impressions. Thoughtful comments on others' posts are half your reach.

---

## Cadence (fits 12–15 hrs/wk)

- **2–3 posts/week + daily thoughtful comments.** The jump from 1 → 2–5 posts/week is the biggest marginal gain.
- **One 90-min weekly batch block** (part of your Sunday ritual): turn the week's breadcrumbs into all posts, schedule them.
- **80/20:** 80% engaging with others in your niche, 20% original posting.
- **Post from the work, not on top of it:** every session, save one artifact (a metric, a screenshot, a gotcha). That's a post.

## Beyond LinkedIn (compounding assets)

- **GitHub profile README** = your real portfolio. Pin the anchor 3. Green CI badges. Clear per-project READMEs.
- **A one-page portfolio site** (GitHub Pages) — demos, repros, posts, contact. Built in W23.
- **Cross-post reproduction writeups** to a blog / dev.to; consider the **NeurIPS 2026 ML Reproducibility Challenge** for a citable credential.
- **Open source:** a few well-scoped PRs to a popular LLM/eval library (LangChain, RAGAS, DeepEval, llama-index) — extends your GSoC signal and puts your name in maintainers' view.

## Converting to freelance (late Dec → 2027)

- **Braintrust** (usebraintrust.com) — most freelancer-favorable: flat **15% client-side fee, you keep 100%.** Best home base.
- **Upwork** — largest, but the generic "AI/ML" pool is over-fished. **Win by niching** (e.g. "RAG + LLM evaluation for [vertical]") — specific beats generic.
- **Gun.io / Toptal** — curated, higher rates, heavier vetting/markup. Worth applying once you have the anchor 3.
- **Positioning > rate-cutting.** Sell outcomes and reliability, not hours. Aim to package (fixed-scope RAG build, an "LLM eval audit" productized service) rather than bill hourly.
- Your **"LLM eval audit"** could be a signature productized offer — cheap to deliver, high-trust, leads to bigger builds. Few competitors offer it. This is the wedge turned into revenue.
